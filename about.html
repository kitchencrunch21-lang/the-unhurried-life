Clara Bennett

Writer & Slow Living Enthusiast

Clara Bennett is a writer based in the American Midwest who covers the quieter side of modern life: cooking at home, spending with intention, and protecting the weekends. Her work has appeared in regional lifestyle magazines and community papers, where she writes a long-running column on simple home routines.

Before writing full time, Clara spent a decade in nonprofit program coordination, a job that taught her two things she still uses every day: how to stretch a small budget without misery, and how to plan a gathering where everyone actually relaxes, including the host. She cooks the same five dinners on rotation, owns one good cast-iron skillet, and believes the best weekends are the ones with nothing scheduled after ten in the morning.

She started The Unhurried Life as a personal notebook and kept it going because readers kept writing in to say the same thing: that ordinary days feel better when you stop trying to optimize them. When she is not writing, she is usually at the farmers market, refolding the same linen napkins, or taking the long way home on purpose.
